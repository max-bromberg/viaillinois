## What is VIA?

VIA (Virtually Integrated Agenda) is a centralized event hub for UIUC ECE student organizations. Instead of hunting across individual club social pages to find out what's happening, you can browse every upcoming RSO event in one place, filter by organization or date, and add things to your calendar.

## What's available today

- **Events feed**: browse and filter upcoming ECE RSO events
- **Calendar view**: see events laid out week-by-week or month-by-month
- **Midterms schedule**: confirmed exam dates pulled from course data

## What's coming

- RSVP and attendance tracking
- Smart venue recommender for RSO event planners
- *And more! Have suggestions? Let us know.*
