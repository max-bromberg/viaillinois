## What VIA is

VIA (Virtually Integrated Agenda) is one place to find what the student organizations of
the Electrical and Computer Engineering department at Illinois have on. Instead of reading
half a dozen club pages to work out what is happening this week, you can browse every
upcoming event in one feed, filter it by organization or by date, and take what you want
into your own calendar.

## What is here today

- The event feed, where you can browse and filter what is coming up.
- The calendar, which lays the same events out by week or by month.
- The midterm schedule, with confirmed exam dates read from course data.

## What is coming

- The scheduler, which suggests a venue to a board planning an event.
- More of the same, and the About page is where to ask for something in particular.

This page stays up to date as new work lands. If something looks broken, or you have an
idea for the platform, the About page has a form for reporting it and an address to write
to.
